--- WHY NO WARNING WAS PRODUCED (verified separately) ---
-Wconversion/-Wsign-conversion (not included in -Wall -Wextra) warn only
about IMPLICIT conversions. Every narrowing/sign-changing conversion in
vulnerable.c uses an EXPLICIT cast, e.g. (short)big_value or
(unsigned int)neg_value. An explicit cast tells the compiler "the
programmer intended this," so the warning is suppressed even when
-Wconversion is added. Verified with a minimal test file:

  short truncated = big_value;             // IMPLICIT -> warns
  short truncated = (short)big_value;      // EXPLICIT -> silent

--- ANALYSIS ---
-Wall -Wextra catch a wide range of mistakes (unused variables, missing
return values, suspicious comparisons, etc.) but they do NOT catch
narrowing or sign-changing conversions once the programmer has written
an explicit cast. A defect deliberately hidden behind a cast is
completely invisible to this configuration.
